# Day 40 – Dashboard & Reporting Experience Design

## Objective

Design the Dashboard and Reporting Experience for MoM Insight 360.

The goal is to transform AI-generated insights into intuitive visual experiences that help management make faster and better decisions.

---

# Why Dashboard Design Matters

Data alone does not create value.

Reports alone do not create value.

Value is created when decision-makers can quickly understand:

- What happened?
- Why it happened?
- What may happen next?
- What actions should be taken?

A well-designed dashboard delivers these answers clearly.

# Dashboard Design Principles

## Simplicity

Display only the most important metrics.

## Clarity

Use clear visualizations and labels.

## Consistency

Maintain a standard layout across reports.

## Actionability

Every dashboard should lead to decisions.

---

# Executive Dashboard

Purpose:

Provide a high-level business overview.

Key Metrics:

- Revenue
- Revenue Growth %
- Scan Count
- Appointment Count
- Forecast Revenue
- Budget vs Actual
- Operational Profit

---
