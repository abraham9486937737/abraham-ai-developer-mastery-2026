# Day 34 – Forecast Agent Design

## Objective

Design the Forecast Intelligence Agent for MoM Insight 360.

The Forecast Agent predicts future business performance using historical data, trends, growth patterns, and KPI behavior.

Its purpose is to help management proactively plan and make better decisions before problems occur.

---

## Business Questions

1. What will next week's revenue be?
2. What will next month's revenue be?
3. Which branch is expected to grow?
4. Which branch may miss targets?
5. What scan volumes can be expected?
6. What referral performance can be expected?
7. What doctor contribution trends are expected?
8. Which KPIs require early management intervention?

## Enterprise Perspective

The Forecast Agent transforms historical business data into future business insights.

Instead of asking:

"What happened?"

Management can ask:

"What is likely to happen next?"

This enables proactive decision-making rather than reactive management.
