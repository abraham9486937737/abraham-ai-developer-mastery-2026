## Objective

The Revenue Agent is responsible for analyzing revenue performance across branches, doctors, referrals, and services.

The goal is to transform raw billing data into meaningful business insights and recommendations for management.

## Business Questions

The Revenue Agent should answer:

1. What is the total revenue generated?

2. Which branch generated the highest revenue?

3. Which branch generated the lowest revenue?

4. What is the month-over-month revenue trend?

5. Which doctor generated the highest revenue?

6. Which referral source generated the highest revenue?

7. Which service or scan contributes the most revenue?

8. Is revenue increasing or decreasing?

9. Which branches need management attention?

10. What opportunities exist to improve revenue?

## Revenue KPI Catalog

| KPI | Description | Formula |
|------|-------------|----------|
| Total Revenue | Total billing revenue generated | SUM(Revenue) |
| Revenue Growth % | Revenue increase/decrease compared to previous period | ((Current - Previous) / Previous) * 100 |
| Revenue per Branch | Revenue generated by each branch | SUM(Revenue) BY Branch |
| Revenue per Doctor | Revenue generated by each doctor | SUM(Revenue) BY Doctor |
| Revenue per Referral | Revenue generated by each referral source | SUM(Revenue) BY Referral |
| Revenue per Scan | Revenue generated by each scan/service | SUM(Revenue) BY Scan |
| Revenue Contribution % | Contribution percentage | Revenue / Total Revenue * 100 |
| Average Revenue per Patient | Revenue generated per patient | Total Revenue / Total Patients |
| Revenue Variance | Difference between actual and target revenue | Actual - Target |
| Revenue Trend | Revenue movement over time | Monthly Analysis |
