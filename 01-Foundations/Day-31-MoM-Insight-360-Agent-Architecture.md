## AI Developer Mastery 2026

### Topic

Designing an Enterprise Multi-Agent Architecture for Healthcare Business Intelligence

## Objective

Convert traditional Business Intelligence dashboards into an Agentic AI Decision Support System.

---

## Current Situation

Traditional BI Systems provide:

- Reports
- Dashboards
- KPIs
- Charts

Users must interpret the information manually.

---

## Vision

MoM Insight 360 should evolve into an AI-powered Executive Intelligence Platform.

Instead of showing only data:

The system should:

- Analyze
- Explain
- Predict
- Recommend

---

## Proposed Multi-Agent Architecture

### Revenue Agent

Responsibilities:

- Revenue Analysis
- Branch Performance
- Growth Trends
- Revenue Variance

Output:

- Revenue Insights
- Revenue Risks
- Revenue Opportunities

---

### KPI Agent

Responsibilities:

- Scan KPIs
- Referral KPIs
- Doctor KPIs
- PRO KPIs

Output:

- KPI Summary
- KPI Exceptions
- KPI Trends

---

### Forecast Agent

Responsibilities:

- Revenue Forecast
- Scan Forecast
- Referral Forecast

Output:

- Future Revenue
- Growth Expectations
- Target Achievement Prediction

---

### Validation Agent

Responsibilities:

- Data Quality Checks
- Missing Data Detection
- KPI Validation

Output:

- Data Quality Score
- Validation Report
- Data Issues

---

### Executive Reporting Agent

Responsibilities:

- Consolidate Agent Outputs
- Generate Executive Summary
- Recommend Actions

Output:

- Executive Dashboard
- Strategic Recommendations
- Decision Support Report

---

Revenue Agent
