Day 29 – Product Intelligence Agent
Objective

Build a Product Intelligence Agent capable of analyzing product performance, categories, brands, profitability, ratings, and identifying high-value products for business decision-making.

What I Learned

Today I learned how Product Intelligence plays a critical role in business analytics and AI-powered decision support systems.

A Product Intelligence Agent helps organizations answer questions such as:

Which products generate the most revenue?
Which products generate the most profit?
Which categories perform best?
Which brands dominate sales?
Which products should be promoted?
Which products should be discontinued?
